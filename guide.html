How the website works

This site follows the classic Topaz-Vbe layout: eight competing companies market three products (P1, P2, P3) in Export, South, West and North. You enter quarterly decisions on the Decisions page. When every required field is filled, the Reports page shows the management accounts.

The business environment

Demand responds to price, advertising, trade press, merchandising, sales coverage and product improvement. Production is manufacture plus assembly. Machine hours and assembly minutes constrain output; overtime is charged when worker hours fall short. Cash is constrained by debtors (credit days), creditors, capital spending and dividends.

Account identities

Revenue = units × regional price. Cost of sales = direct materials + direct labour + production overheads. Gross profit = revenue − cost of sales. Net profit is after selling costs, administration, interest and tax.

FAQs

Why won’t the report generate? Every required field must contain a value. Zero is allowed; a blank is not. Use Fill Sample / Benchmark Data on the Decisions page.

Are teams independent? Yes. All eight companies are stored in this browser’s localStorage.

Print / PDF: Open Reports, then Print / Export PDF and choose “Save as PDF”.
