Eight competing companies. Three products. Four markets. Submit quarterly marketing, operations, personnel and finance decisions, then generate a full management report — in the browser, saved locally.

The 8 strategist roles

Every company is run by a different business strategy. Decisions and reports are pre-filled from each role, so the class can compare how strategies compete — then teams adjust and re-run. Open any team from the Teams menu.

View league table for all 8 companies

What you manage each quarter

Marketing

Prices, advertising, trade press, merchandising and major product improvements for P1–P3.

Operations

Sales coverage, research, materials, suppliers, machines, maintenance and deliveries.

Personnel

Recruit, dismiss and train sales and assembly staff; set wages, shifts and commission.

Finance

Credit policy, dividends, van fleet, management budget and the published accounts.
